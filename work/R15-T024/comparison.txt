3. INVARIANT VERIFICATION RESULTS
---------------------------------
Executed: check_pipeline_invariant(fixture, result)
Result: PASSED (Returns True)

Verified Invariant Properties:
  1. Sequential Step Numbering: Every trace step has sequential step_number starting at 1.
  2. Outcome Completeness: Every input has exactly 1 trace record (4 steps for 4 inputs).
  3. Independent State Reconstruction & Full Snapshot Content Verification:
     For every trace step, the checker independently reconstructs the expected processing state:
     - Verifies raw_input matches input at that index.
     - Verifies outcome status matches reconstructed expectation.
     - Verifies cumulative counts: len(accepted) + len(duplicates) + len(rejected) == k.
     - Verifies that intermediate accepted_so_far, duplicates_so_far, and rejected_so_far match
       the reconstructed lists completely, detecting content replacement even if list length is unchanged.
     - Verifies intermediate accepted list adheres to sorting rule.
  4. Final State Agreement: Result accepted/duplicates/rejected agree with Step 4 snapshots.
  5. Classification Consistency: Accepted/duplicate/rejected counts match trace outcome counts.
  6. Accepted Field Validity: All accepted messages conform to non-empty str device_id and finite numeric values.
  7. Uniqueness of Identity: No duplicate (device_id, timestamp) pairs exist in accepted list.
  8. Deterministic Chronological Sorting: Strictly sorted by (timestamp, device_id, reading).
